♠♥♦♣The Quick Version

  • Your scores, progress, and settings are stored only on your device.
  • There are no accounts and no cloud storage.
  • The game does not send your scores, progress, or settings to us or to any third party.
  • The only thing that collects information is our optional feedback form, which is run by Tally.

Overview

Hazy Outpost built Big Card Blast with your privacy in mind. This Service is provided by Hazy Outpost and is intended for use as is. No warranty is expressed or implied.

This page explains our policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you play the game or visit this website.

Game Data Stays on Your Device

Everything the game remembers, such as your scores, statistics, progress, unlocks, and settings, is saved locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of it on our servers or in the cloud, and we have no way to see it.

The game does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to play.

No Ads, Trackers, or Analytics

Big Card Blast does not include third-party advertising, tracking tools, or analytics services that collect information about you or how you play.

Device Backups

If you use your device's built-in backup features, such as iCloud backup on iOS or Android Auto Backup, your game data may be included in those backups. This is:

  • Entirely optional and controlled by your device settings
  • Governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policies
  • Not accessible to or controlled by Hazy Outpost

We do not access, control, or have any visibility into data backed up through these services.

Security and Your Responsibility

Because your game data is stored on your device, its security depends on your device's security. We recommend keeping your device updated and protected with a lock screen. While we take reasonable care, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The game itself collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Our feedback form is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before submitting the form. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights and Choices

  • You can delete all game data at any time by uninstalling the game.
  • You can manage device backups through your device settings.
  • You can ask us to delete a feedback submission by contacting us below.
  • You own and control the data stored on your device.

Feedback Form (Tally)

Our feedback page uses an embedded form from Tally (tally.so). Using it is completely optional. If you choose to submit it:

  • We receive whatever you type into the form, such as your comments and any contact details you choose to include.
  • If you open the form from within the game, it also includes your device platform (for example, iOS or Android) and the game version, so we can make sense of your feedback.
  • We use your feedback only to understand how to improve the game and, if you asked for a reply, to respond to you.
  • Tally processes the submission on our behalf and is governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Loading the form means your browser connects to Tally's servers, so Tally may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details.

Please don't include sensitive personal information in your feedback. No scores, progress, or other gameplay data is ever included with a submission.

App Store Information

We may receive anonymized, aggregated information from the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, such as download counts and crash reports. This information does not identify individual players and helps us keep the game stable.

Links to Other Sites

This website may link to other sites that we don't operate. We have no control over their content or privacy practices, so please review their policies if you visit them.
